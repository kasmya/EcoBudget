B1  -  New contribution: Energy-Aware Stopping Reward

The stopping controller previously optimized success minus a byte penalty. Bytes on the extracted snippets are ~102 B, so that penalty is nearly free - the policy barely feels retrieval cost. We reformulated the reward to charge each retrieval its true 5G energy: a fetch wakes the radio (RRC promotion + active + inactivity tail), a fixed ~107 J per page fetch under fast-dormancy. The policy now stops to save joules, not bytes.

B2  -  New contribution: Carbon-Intensity-Aware Scheduling

Joules become carbon via grid intensity - which is not constant: it swings ~2.3× over a day (deep midday solar trough, sharp evening peak). The same query costs very different carbon depending on when it runs. For latency-tolerant agent retrieval we shift each query to the greenest hour within a slack window. This is a time axis orthogonal to Idea 1, and the savings stack on top of it.

keep02 RESEARCH GAP
Retrieval research optimizes relevance ranking (what to load), not task-sufficiency (how much to load); and "green AI" work rarely models the 5G access path (RRC radio-state / tail energy) or the time-varying carbon of the grid.
keepRESEARCH QUESTION
Can an agent retrieve only the evidence a task requires - and stop when enough is enough - to cut data transfer, energy, and carbon without losing task success over 5G?
keepHYPOTHESIS
Requirement-aware retrieval plus a learned stopping policy holds full-retrieval task success while transferring substantially fewer bytes and joules.
keep03 ECOBUDGET APPROACH
Retrieve only the evidence needed to complete the task, and stop when extra retrieval adds no value: (1) understand the task's information requirements; (2) retrieve targeted to those; (3) measure evidence sufficiency vs data already transferred; (4) STOP or RETRIEVE; (5) balance success against cost.

  • Evidence sufficiency (RoBERTa QA-confidence per requirement, not raw similarity).
  • Adaptive stopping controller: STOP or RETRIEVE, trained on a measured-5G-energy reward.
  • Answer generation from only the gathered evidence.
  • (new) Carbon-aware scheduler: defer latency-tolerant queries to low-carbon grid windows.
